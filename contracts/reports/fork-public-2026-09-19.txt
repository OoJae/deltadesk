Does the public RPC work? Yes at latest, no at a pinned block.
- It serves state only for roughly the last 5,000-20,000 blocks (about 30 minutes at ~100 ms blocks): eth_call at
  latest-5000 answered, at latest-20000 it returned "historical state ... is not available". A fork pinned at the
  default WEEKDAY_BLOCK (66,315,359 = Fri 2026-09-18 15:00:00 UTC) or SATURDAY_BLOCK (66,851,211 = Sat 2026-09-19
  06:00:00 UTC) therefore needs the archive RPC (Alchemy); both numbers were found by bisection over block headers,
  which the public RPC does serve.
- At latest the whole suite ran in 90 s, well inside the retention window, with no rate limiting observed.

ForkLifecycle (the block is a Saturday, so the test moved to Monday 02:00 UTC and re-stamped the live feed rounds)

  - a whale moved the pool +-60 ticks three times: the lane collected 31,624 USDG base units and 0.000142 NVDA in fees
